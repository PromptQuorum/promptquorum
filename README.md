> ## What is PromptQuorum?
>
> PromptQuorum lets you send a single prompt to 25+ leading AI models simultaneously — GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini, Mistral, LLaMA, and more — and instantly see a **consensus verdict** across all responses.
>
> Stop guessing which AI gives the best answer. Let them vote.
>
> ---
>
> ## Features
>
> - **25+ Models at once** — Run your prompt across OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Mistral, and more in a single click
> - - **Consensus Verdict** — PromptQuorum aggregates and compares outputs to surface the most agreed-upon answer
>   - - **Side-by-side Comparison** — See every model's response in a clean, scannable layout
>     - - **Prompt History** — Save, revisit, and refine your prompts over time
>       - - **Fast & Clean UI** — Designed for power users who need answers, not friction
>         - - **EU-based & Privacy-first** — Built in Germany, designed with data privacy in mind
